# Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 35 of 2019)

- **Authority**: Ministry of Law and Justice / Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Government of India
- **Official Source URL**: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Scope**: Consumer protection across all goods and services throughout India.

## Key Provisions & Statutory Rights

### 1. Consumer Rights (Section 2(9))
Includes:
- The right to be protected against marketing of goods, products, or services that are hazardous to life and property.
- The right to be informed about the quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard, and price of goods or services.
- The right to be assured of access to a variety of goods or services at competitive prices.
- The right to be heard and assured that consumer interests will receive due consideration.
- The right to seek redressal against unfair trade practices or restrictive trade practices or unscrupulous exploitation of consumers.

### 2. Deficiency in Service (Section 2(11))
"Deficiency" means any fault, imperfection, shortcoming, or inadequacy in the quality, nature, and manner of performance that is required to be maintained by or under any law or under a contract.

### 3. Unfair Trade Practice (Section 2(47))
Includes false representation that goods are of a particular standard or quality, non-issuance of bill/cash memo, refusing to take back defective goods or refund money within the period stipulated.

### 4. Direct Online Filing via e-Daakhil (Section 35)
Consumers can file digital complaints directly before District, State, or National Consumer Commissions through the e-Daakhil portal (`https://edaakhil.nic.in`) without needing physical appearance or legal counsel.
