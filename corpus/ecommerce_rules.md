# Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

- **Authority**: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Government of India
- **Notification**: G.S.R. 462(E), dated 23rd July, 2020
- **Official Source URL**: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Status**: VERIFIED against official gazette notification

## Key Statutory Provisions & Consumer Rights

### 1. Mandatory Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Officer (Rule 4(4))
Every e-commerce entity operating in India is mandated under **Rule 4(4)** to:
- Establish an adequate grievance redressal mechanism appropriate to the volume of consumer grievances.
- Appoint a designated **Grievance Officer** for consumer dispute resolution.
- Display prominently on its website / mobile application the name, designation, and direct contact details (including email address and telephone) of such Grievance Officer.

### 2. Statutory Redressal Timelines (Rule 4(5))
Under **Rule 4(5)**, every e-commerce entity shall ensure that its Grievance Officer:
- **Acknowledges receipt** of any consumer complaint within **forty-eight (48) hours**.
- **Redresses the complaint** within **one (1) month (30 days)** from the date of receipt of the complaint.

### 3. Return, Refund, and Unfair Trade Practices (Rule 4(10) & Rule 6)
- **Rule 4(10)**: No e-commerce entity shall impose cancellation charges on consumers after confirming purchase unless similar charges are borne by the entity upon cancellation.
- Delivery of defective, deficient, or counterfeit goods constitutes deficiency in service under Section 2(11) and an unfair trade practice under Section 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

### 4. Statutory Escalation Pathways
If the entity fails to acknowledge within 48 hours or resolve within 30 days:
- **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)**: Ingrams portal (`https://consumerhelpline.gov.in`) or National Toll-Free `1915`.
- **Consumer Commission Filing**: Direct online case filing through the **e-Daakhil** portal (`https://edaakhil.nic.in`) under Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
- **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)**: Complaint under Section 20 of CPA 2019 for widespread unfair trade practices.
