# Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS)

- **Authority**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- **Official Source URL**: https://cms.rbi.org.in
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Scope**: All Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Non-Bank System Participants (UPI, Wallets, Payment Aggregators).

## Key Provisions & Statutory Rights

### 1. Single Window Redressal
Covers deficiency in service such as:
- Unauthorized electronic debit or failed UPI/ATM transaction where account was debited without money dispensed/credited.
- Levy of hidden or unauthorized charges without prior notice.
- Refusal or inordinate delay in closing accounts or processing chargebacks.

### 2. Statutory Prerequisite & Timelines (RB-IOS 2021 Clause 10)
Under **Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021, Clause 10**:
- **Mandatory First Step**: The complainant must first lodge a formal complaint with the regulated financial entity (Bank / NBFC / Payment App).
- **Escalation Window**: If the entity:
  - Fails to reply within **thirty (30) days** from receipt of complaint, OR
  - Rejects the complaint, OR
  - Provides an unsatisfactory reply,
  the consumer has the statutory right to immediately escalate to the RBI Ombudsman online via the Complaint Management System (CMS) at `https://cms.rbi.org.in`.

### 3. Compensation & Awards (RB-IOS 2021 Clause 15)
Under **RB-IOS 2021, Clause 15**:
- The Ombudsman is empowered to award compensation up to **₹20,00,000** for actual direct consequential loss caused to the complainant.
- In addition, the Ombudsman may award compensation up to **₹1,00,000** for loss of complainant's time, expenses incurred, and mental agony/harassment.
