# DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) - Passenger Rights

- **Authority**: Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India
- **Regulation**: CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV (Rev. 3) - Facilitation to passengers due to denied boarding, flight cancellation, and flight delay.
- **Official Source URL**: https://www.dgca.gov.in/digigov-portal/?page=4056/4053/servicename
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Scope**: All scheduled and non-scheduled commercial flights operating within or departing from India.

## Key Provisions & Statutory Rights

### 1. Refund of Airline Ticket (Section 3, Series M, Part II & IV)
- In case of flight cancellation or delay exceeding the statutory threshold (over 2 hours for short flights, over 4 hours for long flights):
  - **Credit Card payments**: Full refund must be processed and completed by the airline within **seven (7) working days**.
  - **Cash payments**: Immediate refund across the counter.
  - **Travel Agent / Portal bookings**: Airline must process refund to the agent who must refund the passenger within 30 days.

### 2. Baggage Loss / Damage (The Carriage by Air Act, 1972)
- Compensation for lost, delayed, or damaged checked luggage is governed by the statutory liability limit (up to ₹20,000 per passenger on domestic sectors, or Montreal Convention SDR limits on international flights).
- A Property Irregularity Report (PIR) must be filed within 7 days for damage, 21 days for delay.

### 3. Escalation Route
- Level 1: Airline Nodal / Appellate Officer.
- Level 2: Ministry of Civil Aviation "AirSewa" portal (`https://airsewa.gov.in`) or DGCA passenger grievance cell.
- Level 3: National Consumer Helpline (`1915` / `consumerhelpline.gov.in`).
