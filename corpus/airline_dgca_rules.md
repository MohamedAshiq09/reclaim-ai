# DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) - Passenger Rights & Ticket Refunds

- **Authority**: Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India
- **Core Ticket Refund Regulation**: **CAR Section 3, Series M, Part II** (Title: *Refund of Airline Tickets to Passengers of Public Transport Undertakings*)
- **Facilities & Compensation Regulation**: **CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV** (Title: *Facilities to be provided to passengers by airlines due to denied boarding, cancellation of flights and delays in flights*)
- **Official Source URL**: https://www.dgca.gov.in/digigov-portal/?page=4056/4053/servicename
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Status**: VERIFIED against DGCA CAR Section 3 Series M

## Key Statutory Provisions & Refund Timelines

### 1. Ticket Refund Timelines (CAR Section 3, Series M, Part II - Para 3.3)
Under **Part II, Para 3.3.2**:
- **Credit Card Bookings**: The airline must process and complete the refund within **seven (7) working days** of cancellation to the credit card account.
- **Cash Bookings**: Refund must be made **immediately** across the ticket counter.
- **Travel Agent / Online Travel Portals (OTAs)**: Under DGCA directives and CAR revisions, airlines must refund through the portal/agent within **fourteen (14) working days**, and the intermediary must disburse directly to the passenger without delay.

### 2. Flight Cancellation & Delay Compensation (CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV)
Under **Part IV**:
- If a flight is cancelled or delayed without adequate prior intimation (minimum 24 hours):
  - Passengers are entitled to full refund OR alternate flight without additional charge.
  - In addition, compensation between ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 is mandated based on block time if airlines fail to inform in time.
  - Meals and refreshments must be provided for delays exceeding 2 hours.

### 3. Baggage Damage / Loss
- Governed by the **Carriage by Air Act, 1972** and notifications thereunder:
  - Domestic liability limit is capped at statutory rates (up to ₹20,000 per passenger).
  - Passenger must lodge a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) before leaving the arrival terminal.

### 4. Statutory Escalation Pathways
- **Level 1**: Airline Nodal Officer / Grievance Redressal Officer.
- **Level 2**: Ministry of Civil Aviation **AirSewa Portal** (`https://airsewa.gov.in`).
- **Level 3**: **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)** (`1915` / `https://consumerhelpline.gov.in`) or **e-Daakhil** Consumer Commission filing.
